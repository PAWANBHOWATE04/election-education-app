Voter Registration

Unprepared

Voter registration is the essential first step in exercising your democratic rights. In this phase, you must verify your eligibility, prepare required proof of identity and residence, and submit your registration details online or through paper forms.

Pro-Tip for Citizens

Always register well ahead of time. Many jurisdictions enforce a voter cutoff date generally 15-30 days before Election Day. Make sure to double-check spelling of your address on your submitted forms.
